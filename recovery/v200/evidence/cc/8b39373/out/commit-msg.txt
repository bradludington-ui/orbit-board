v2.00 (U36): an RCN / EEIC title over 80 characters is refused, never cut

The registry-text investigation found that rcnSet cut a typed RCN / EEIC title to its first
80 UTF-16 units and said nothing. 81 letters were kept as 80. 79 letters and an emoji were kept as
79 letters and half the emoji, which RCN-EEIC by Month printed as a replacement mark and the saved
file kept. Any edit of a title that arrived in a file longer than 80 cut the file's title the same
way.

The title now follows item 91's rule for codes. It is refused: the list keeps what it had, nothing
is saved, the field keeps what was typed, marked (the panel's title field now passes itself to
rcnSet), and the status says "RCN / EEIC title not changed". Once a value is then kept in the list,
that refusal leaves the status line, so the line never says "not changed" of a title that has just
changed; any other message stays. The limit counts Unicode code points: an emoji stored as two
halves counts once; a joined emoji, a flag or a letter with a separate accent mark counts as
several.
