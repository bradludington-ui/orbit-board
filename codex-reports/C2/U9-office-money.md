# U9: define an office’s funded money once

**Decision requested.** Should an office’s funded figure follow the CLIN amounts actually exported by service, funding lane and award date, or remain a separate planning apportionment? Recommend the former, while retaining the office’s requested amount as a distinct quantity.

**What is established.** The pinned ledger reports the U9 exact-split defect: at weights 1:2:3, $3 should be $1/$1/$1, and $9 should be $2/$3/$4. Batch B1 independently confirmed the snapshot’s arithmetic; that does not settle this broader policy. The status file reports disagreements with partial funding, two CLINs assigned to one office, board-added CLINs and incremental awards. No fresh runtime reproduction is claimed here. Source inspection at base `7ee498a` shows two allocation paths: `pmoPartsOf` allocates across offices, whereas `srcRowsOf` splits each funded lane and dated amount through `clinSplit`. The source exports **both discretionary and mandatory**, on separate lines. Its current PMO column is the requirement’s home office (`grpOf(it)`), so grouping that column alone cannot recover CLIN-office ownership.

**Options and consequences.**

1. **Keep separate planning and export interpretations.** Label the office amount “planning share”; give exported CLIN amounts a separate label and a reconciliation. Existing entries and work remain intact, and requirement totals can still tie. An office nevertheless has two answers, so the board must never present the planning share as the money assigned to its CLINs.
2. **Make funded office figures the roll-up of the actual CLIN allocations.** Allocate each service/lane/date amount once, using exact whole dollars and the agreed tie order, then sum the CLINs assigned to each office. All funded office panels and exports consume that same attribution. A CLIN not backed by an office assignment needs an explicit rule, such as its requirement’s home office; a missing office row must not drop its money. Dates, lane budgets and entered CLIN values stay unchanged. Existing displayed office ownership can move by whole dollars, which requires a before/after reconciliation rather than silent normalization.
3. **Change the Spend Plan Source to match today’s office-first split.** This preserves office panels but changes exported CLIN ownership and potentially the amounts on particular lane/date lines. It needs its own financial policy approval and receiving-system checks. It is the larger change to the export contract.

**Why the distinction matters.** As a policy illustration, a requirement asks for $2 across two equally weighted CLINs owned by offices A and B: the planning ask splits $1/$1. Funding is $1 discretionary plus $1 mandatory. A capacity-first split can assign funded $1/$1; independent lane ties can instead assign funded A $2 / B $0. Both sum to the same $2, but A now has $1 more funding than its apportioned ask. A naive ask-minus-funded would produce A −$1 / B +$1; flooring both would falsely report $1 unfunded when the requirement has $0 unfunded. These are hand-written illustrative literals, not a new ORBIT test result. “Split each lane separately” therefore needs a precise CLIN, office and award rule, not just a different rounding call.

**Recommendation and acceptance criteria.** Choose option 2 for **funded** office money. Preserve ask, allocation, directed funding and unfunded as separately named quantities; do not rewrite them to make an export match. The capacity tradeoff must be explicit: choose whether actual source CLIN ownership may exceed an office’s apportioned planning ask, or require a newly approved capacity-constrained CLIN rule shared by source and panels. Both guarantees cannot be assumed together. Under the recommended source-ownership policy, retain the entered ask and show any signed funded-versus-planning-ask variance clearly; never hide an above-ask office, silently rewrite ask, or call a negative residual “unfunded”. Keep authoritative requirement/service unfunded separate rather than summing floored office residuals. Before adoption, independently prove every service/lane/date/CLIN dollar is assigned once, requirement and lane totals are preserved, and each office’s funded total equals its canonical CLIN roll-up. Cover both lanes, partial funding, same-office CLINs, added CLINs and increments with literal dollar expectations and actual exported rows. U43’s omitted office row is a separate open completeness defect: this decision does not close it, but the acceptance matrix must include it.
