## How to read it

| status | means |
|---|---|
| `proven·A` | proven to the dollar by checks that compute what they expect independently — from the fixture's own numbers, or an identity that must hold whatever the code does (**arithmetic**) |
| `proven·G` | proven to the dollar by checks that read what they expect from the board's own helpers (tiers(), crData(), forecast() …): the surface **agrees** with the board, which is right only if the helper is |
| `proven·AG` | both kinds of check |
| `·d` | proven at the surface's own display precision (the deck: fSM), not to the dollar |
| `n/a` | the surface does not carry that dimension (the reason is given) |
| **OPEN** | not proven: what is missing, what closing it takes, listed below for Brad to build or defer |

| dimension | carried when | proven when |
|---|---|---|
| **Appropriation** | the surface prints money for more than one appropriation, or reads another appropriation's board or rows | each appropriation's figures are held to that appropriation's own board or data |
| **Fiscal year** | the surface prints more than one fiscal year, or reads a year other than the one on screen (a lock, another year's state, imported rows) | each year's figures are held to that year's own state, on a board where the years differ |
| **Service** | any figure is broken out by service (a row or a column per service) | every per-service figure, at every grain it is printed (per requirement, per office, in total), is held |
| **Requirement** | any figure is printed for one requirement | every requirement's figures are held, and the right requirements are listed |
| **Month** | money is placed in fiscal months (October–September, UNSCHEDULED, an obligation Month or Date) | every month's figure is held, from the fixture's award dates or the one placement rule |
| **CR half** | money is placed in the twelve October–March half-month windows | every window's figure is held, from each award's own date |
| **Funding lane** | discretionary and mandatory are stated apart (drawn, budget authority, allocated, a lane's own line) | every lane-specific figure is held |

## Found while mapping

### F1. The workbook writer keeps two decimals, so a sheet printed in $M or $K is not whole dollars in the file

buildXlsx writes every non-integer number as Math.round(v*100)/100. Sheets in dollars are unaffected (their figures are whole). Three are not in dollars: the Two-Plan Forecast ($M → to $10,000), the ATR/BTR log ($M as typed → to $10,000) and the 1A9A CR cash return ($K → to $10). Their figures are right before the writer; the file holds the rounding. The deck is the same kind of thing by design (fSM) and is mapped at its display precision. Fixed in v1.95 (item 60): buildXlsx stores the figure as it is (to 15 significant digits, Excel's own precision) and the cell's number format keeps the display; a fraction in a style with no format gets that style's two-decimal twin. Proven by w7.f1 (the three files, to the dollar) and w7.writer. (The ATR/BTR log was removed with its tracker in v2.02.)

*Observed:* 3 Oct 2026, the files read back with the board's own reader (xlsxRead): the Two-Plan Forecast's SE line, 0.085069 on the sheet, is 0.09 in the file; a reprogramming entry typed 1.2345 ($M) is 1.23; the 1A9A cells for $124,458 and $85,069 are 124.46 and 85.07 ($K).

*Cells it keeps open or qualifies:* exportExcel × month and × lane (Two-Plan Forecast), qualifies crExportRows × CR half.

### F2. The Spend Plan Source dates an incremental award's whole line by its first increment as stored

srcRowsOf takes when = aw.inc[0].d (the first increment in the array, unsorted) and the day from the award object (which an increment does not carry, so the 1st). All of the line's money is dated there, though the increments land in other months. v1.94 fixed the same rule for the 1A9A OBL DATE (crObl sorts with awdCmp and uses awdDay); the Spend Plan Source was not changed. Fixed in v1.95 (item 60), Brad's option (a): an incremental award goes out a line per increment, each dated the day landSvc() places it on and carrying its own part of each lane; what the awards do not cover goes out on 31 August. Proven by w7.f2.

*Observed:* 3 Oct 2026, srcRowsOf on a board whose award for A holds increments stored March (day 3) then November (day 9): both of A's lines (USAF $300, ANG $100) are dated 1 March 2027; the forecast places USAF $200 and ANG $100 in November and USAF $100 in March.

*Cells it keeps open or qualifies:* srcRowsOf, exportSrc, xl · Spend Plan Source × month.

### F3. The Executive Funding Synopsis is titled FY27 whatever the year

execSynopsis writes 'FY27 EXECUTIVE FUNDING SYNOPSIS' and names the file 'FY27 Exec Funding Synopsis …' as literals; the figures are the year on screen's. Fixed in v1.95 (item 60): FYL() in the first line and the file name. Proven by w7.f3.

*Observed:* 3 Oct 2026, the synopsis exported with FY28 on screen (MS.cur 2028): its first line and its file name both say FY27.

*Cells it keeps open or qualifies:* execSynopsis × fiscal year.

### F4. An office's share of a requirement split by its CLINs is a fraction of a dollar

pmoSharesOf gives each office CLIN-weighted fractions of PRIME-3, per service, and the by-office views multiply the draw by them: the PMO Matrix, the PMO tabs and the forecast by PMO were seen to carry cents (the file writes them to two decimals); the CR panel by PMO uses the same shares (read in the code, not run). The rule for money everywhere else is whole dollars, spread with apportion(). Fixed in v1.95 (item 60): every by-office figure comes from pmoPartsOf(), in whole dollars by apportion() — the ask, then discretionary, mandatory and unfunded, and the months award by award — so a $100 draw split 1:2 is $67 and $33 on every surface. Proven by w7.f4.

*Observed:* 3 Oct 2026, a $100 STOVL draw split by CLINs 1 : 2: AV 66.67 and MxS 33.33 on the PMO Matrix and the PMO MxS tab (read back from the file); forecast().byPMO.MxS = 33.333….

*Cells it keeps open or qualifies:* exportExcel × service, xl · PMO Matrix × service, forecast × month, exportFcView × month.

### F5. A stale footnote on CR Cash Needs (no money effect)

The sheet still says 'Month-dated obligations default to the 1–15 window; flip individual requirements to 16–EOM in the board’s CR view'. Since v1.94 the award's own date decides and nothing flips. Fixed in v1.95 (item 60): the footnote states the v1.94 rule. Held by w7.f5.

*Observed:* in exportExcel's CR sheet builder (read in the source).

*Cells it keeps open or qualifies:* none (wording only).
